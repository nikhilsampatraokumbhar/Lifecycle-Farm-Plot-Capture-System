PRODUCT MANAGEMENT EXERCISE Senior Product Manager (Technical) — AgriTech Exercise Title Lifecycle Farm Plot Capture System Time Allocation 24 hours (take-home) Context & Business Problem You are joining an agritech company that operates a contract farming platform connecting agricultural companies with smallholder farmers. The platform manages the entire contract lifecycle—from farmer onboarding through harvest and settlement. A critical capability gap exists: accurate, ongoing geo-spatial capture of farm plots throughout the crop lifecycle. Currently, field technicians manually estimate areas, leading to: • 15-25% variance between contracted area and actual cultivated area • Disputes during harvest settlement • Inaccurate input allocation (seeds, fertilizers) • Poor yield predictions affecting procurement planning KEY INSIGHT Agriplotting is NOT a one-time activity. Plot boundaries must be captured at multiple stages throughout the crop lifecycle to track actual cultivated area, assess crop health, and reconcile yields. The Assignment Design a mobile-first geo-capture system that enables field technicians to capture and validate farm plot boundaries at multiple stages of the crop lifecycle. The solution must work in low-connectivity rural areas and generate actionable data for multiple downstream use cases.
